Selection and state contract
• Two selection styles, one per layer. Main UI (tiles, popup menus, page selector, profile switcher): surfaceSelected fill, no ring. Settings and set-up flows (menus, rows, controls, picker lists, onboarding) and buttons everywhere: 3px focusRing, no fill.
• The ring goes on the element OK activates: around a list row, on the control of a setting with a switch, slider or field. The label and help text are not marked.
• Selection is bound to ui.keyNavigationActive: invisible after a touch, visible from the first key press.
• Entering a screen with a key preselects the first actionable element; entering by touch preselects nothing.
• The selected element is kept inside the middle 70 percent of the viewport while scrolling.
• Pressed inverts the element: textPrimary fill with black labels and icons. A pressed destructive button is #FF0D2A with a white label.
• Every tappable element is reachable by d-pad, or it is a touch-only enhancement that has a d-pad equivalent on the same screen.

Layout rules
• Page gutter 20px. Content, dividers and buttons align to it. No 0, 10, 30 or 40px variants.
• Selectable rows and tiles are drawn 8px from the screen edge so the selection never touches the bezel; their content keeps the 20px gutter.
• Row heights: 80px single line, 110px with a secondary line, 130px tiles. Title bar 80px.
• Dividers 2px divider colour, inset 20px. Never 1px, never a hairline in surface colour.
• Corner radius 8px on rows, tiles, buttons; 22px on sheets, fields and popups. No literals.
• Hit targets at least 60x60. Icons in three sizes: 60 (rows), 80 (title bar, close), 100 (tiles), glyph half the box.
• Opacity is for overlays (0.85 black) and disabled controls (0.4). Never to de-emphasise text.
• Bottom sheets are black with a 22px top radius and a 2px divider edge over a 0.85 dim.
• Popup menus end with a Close row styled like the other rows, not a button.
